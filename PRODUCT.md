# Product

## Platform
web — static GitHub Pages site.

## Purpose
Public cartoon 3D-print model storefront with categories, product details, a shopping bag and downloadable model packages.

## Confirmed requirements
Remove single-color three-part and eight-part zongzi versions. Retain ghost, Halloween, original-color three-part zongzi, one-piece four-color and one-piece monochrome. Exclude portrait photographs and realistic models from public uploads. Use actual model renders and X1 Carbon print instructions.

## Open decision
User has been asked for free versus paid distribution, prices and payment provider. Until supplied, checkout is explicitly free. No payment processing is represented as active.

## Product variants
Four designs: ghost, pumpkin, zongzi and kitty. Each product exposes one-piece and assembly options. Construction choice and color option must persist through cart and downloads. Kitty torso is wider than the head (84.9 mm versus 71.8 mm) and black hair with white cat ears only; a red bow and no hat. Pricing clarification remains unanswered, so all prices retain the existing free value.
