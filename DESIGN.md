# Store design

Preserve the established cream, forest green and pumpkin orange palette. Product photography is actual 3D mesh rendering. Use airy spacing, serif Chinese headings, quiet sans-serif utility text and warm editorial composition. Homepage hero leads to a four-product collection; categories and search remain directly above products. Product details and shopping bag use native dialogs with Escape and visible close controls. Checkout clearly states free collection and exposes individual ZIP downloads.

Tokens: paper #f7f5ee; ink #233c32; muted #59685e; orange #bd4f26; rule #d6dcd1. Body line-height 1.7. Display uses Noto Serif TC with serif fallback. Responsive product grid: four columns desktop, two tablet/mobile. Native dialog focus, reduced-motion preference and visible keyboard focus are retained.
